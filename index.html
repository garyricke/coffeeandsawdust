How We Work

The Coffee & Sawdust Process

From raw slab to your kitchen — every step is done with intention.

01

Select the Wood

We hand-pick every board from responsibly sourced exotic and domestic hardwoods — Walnut, Maple, Cherry, Purple Heart, and more.

02

Cut & Shape

Using a planer, jointer, band saw, and router table — each piece is cut and shaped to exact dimensions.

03

05

Delivered with Pride

Heat-stamped with our brand mark and ready for your home in 2–3 weeks. Every piece inspected before it leaves the shop.

Our Story

How It All Started

Coffee & Sawdust was born from a tradition Dylan shared with his father — starting each day with a fresh cup of coffee before heading out to their garage woodshop to build something together.

It started in a Minnesota basement. Dylan's grandfather was a computer engineer who did woodworking on the side, passing the craft to Dylan's dad. When the family moved to Batavia, the shop came with them — and it grew. A planer, a jointer, a drum sander, a laser engraver, a router table. One tool at a time, the workshop took shape.

Those early mornings — filled with sawdust, conversation, and craftsmanship — became the foundation for what would eventually grow into Coffee & Sawdust. Dylan started making boards on his own, entered the school's business incubator program, sold at the Rustic Fox Market in Aurora, and kept building from there.

Where We Source Wood

Owl Hardwood, near Addison/Downers Grove, IL — a specialty exotic hardwood supplier carrying Walnut, Maple, Purple Heart, Padauk, Wenge, Yellow Heart, Sapele, White Oak, and more.

Board Care — The Secret

Once a month, apply our homemade beeswax and mineral oil blend. Let it sit for an hour, then buff off. Your board will stay sealed, beautiful, and food-safe for years.

Do Wood Boards Kill Bacteria?

Yes. Hardwood traps and kills bacteria naturally through capillary action — something plastic boards can't do. We also use a food-safe finish on every piece.

Are Boards Mass Produced?

Never. Each board is hand-selected, individually cut, sanded, and finished. Same dimensions, but no two pieces ever look exactly alike — the grain makes every one unique.

Chef's Choice

End-Grain Boards

The most technically demanding boards we make — and the most beautiful. End-grain boards expose the cross-section of the wood fibers, creating a stunning mosaic pattern that's also the most knife-friendly surface you can own.

What is End-Grain?

Instead of cutting with the wood's length, we rotate each piece 90°. The fibers face up like a bundle of straws — your knife slips between them instead of across them, protecting both the blade and the board.

Why It's More Work

Every piece must be squared to 90° on all sides, then glued in precise arrangements. Any variation shows. It takes significantly longer than a face-grain board — and that's reflected in the price.

Wood Combinations

Walnut + Maple is the classic. Purple Heart + Cherry creates stunning contrast. Custom color arrangements available — you choose the woods, we design the pattern.

Pricing

Starting at $250 due to wood cost and labor. Large end-grain boards with exotic woods run $300–$400+. Worth every penny — these last decades.
